# ScoutLIQ — visual system

## Direction

ScoutLIQ is an operational product in the ORLIQ family. The ORLIQ landing defines the quality floor, not the layout template.

- Brand signature: deep crimson, charcoal/near-black canvas, warm off-white typography.
- Parent-brand champagne is reserved for subtle ORLIQ identification.
- Information hierarchy comes from scale, rhythm, spacing and hairlines before containers.
- Glass is limited to contextual layers such as the top bar, popovers and dialogs.
- Native browser-looking controls are replaced by ScoutLIQ controls while preserving semantic form behavior.
- Desktop is designed for 100% browser zoom. Mobile uses an off-canvas navigation and re-composed content rather than a simple stacked desktop layout.

## Composition by area

- Overview: dominant commercial result, operational pulse, territory and trend as one field, priority ledger.
- Radar: integrated map/results workstation with one discovery command line and custom filters.
- Pipeline: vertical stage ledger with horizontal deal tracks; not a six-column kanban.
- Clients: editorial data ledger with clear identity/contact/stage hierarchy.
- Sites: operational portfolio treatment with domain/status/financial hierarchy.
- Finance: results hierarchy led by revenue and period, followed by transaction ledgers.
- Settings: grouped by context with restrained editorial sections and custom controls.
- About: factual product, version, infrastructure and ORLIQ relationship.

## Motion

Transitions are short, spatial and continuous. Reduced-motion preferences disable non-essential transitions and animations. Scroll is never hijacked.

## Non-goals

No neon/glow system, generic SaaS gradients, decorative 3D, card grids for their own sake, motivational copy or visible native browser widgets.
